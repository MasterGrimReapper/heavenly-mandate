# Heavenly Mandate — v0.055 changelog

**Play with friends, in your language, with a master to teach you.**

1. Friends: add a friend by their name (Name#1234 at the top of the Friends panel). Friends see each other online, in a party or in a match. Up to 100 friends
2. Parties: invite online friends into a party of up to 5. The leader picks a queue and a format and the whole party searches together, always on the same team. The party stays together after the match
3. Chat: team, all and party chat with four quick phrases. Messages are filtered for bad words, limited to one a second and never saved in replays. Hold (phones) or click (computers) a name to mute that player
4. Online formats: Ranked, Quick Match, Bridge of Immortals and Duels each ask for a format: 5v5, 3v3, 2v2 or 1v1. Ranked and Quick Match 5v5 play on Three Realms, 3v3 and 2v2 on Twin Rivers, 1v1 on the duel bridge. Team duels end at 10 (5v5), 7 (3v3) or 5 (2v2) takedowns or the first pagoda
5. Smoother movement online: your own immortal is drawn where it is going, the moment you move the stick, instead of after the input delay. The match itself is unchanged; Settings › Online › Predict my movement turns it off
6. Tips show once each, one at a time, and never while an enemy immortal is hitting you. The first tip has a Turn off button (Settings › Gameplay › Tips)
7. Attack button: it now reaches much farther and locks on. It prefers an enemy immortal within reach, otherwise the nearest soldier, monster or structure, and your immortal walks into range. An enemy immortal coming close takes over from a soldier; a second tap switches immortals; the target frame at the top shows who you are attacking (tap it to let go)
8. Bahasa Indonesia: the whole game in Indonesian. On first start the game picks your phone's language and lets you choose English, 简体中文 or Bahasa Indonesia; change it any time in Settings › Gameplay › Language. Only your language's text is loaded
9. First-run lesson: an old master teaches the basics in a voiced, playable lesson of about three minutes (English and Mandarin voices), then walks you through the menu and Settings, then gives five beginner quests with rewards. Skip it with "I know MOBAs"; replay it from Settings › Gameplay
10. Offline play is focused on six modes: Three Realms, Bridge of Immortals, Duel of Immortals, King of the River, Siege of Heaven and the Practice Grounds. The other modes return in later updates
11. Attack lock-on is safe near towers: it never walks you under an enemy pagoda alone (it stops at the edge and says "Enemy pagoda ahead") and never pulls you into a jungle camp from afar. The red ring under the locked target is now drawn
12. Phones and tablets: every battle button has a touch area of at least 40 px (tip Turn off, top buttons, item slots, attack-soldiers and attack-pagoda, enemy portraits); the spirit fox's bubble keeps clear of chat, the target frame and tips; "Why did I die?" follows the Tips switch; map names show in full; the end screen scrolls clear of its buttons; the master's card is never covered by the controls guide; long menu labels wrap; no keyboard or mouse tips on the loading screen

# Heavenly Mandate — v0.054 changelog

**Smooth movement online.**

1. Online, immortals steered with the thumb stick or a gamepad no longer move in stop-and-go steps. The stick now sends a direction the immortal keeps following (as the keyboard always did) instead of a point just ahead, which arrived after the input delay and made every immortal pause each turn. Measured: at a 300 ms input delay the old way stopped the immortal 8% of the time and cut its speed by 22%; the new way is as smooth as offline

# Heavenly Mandate — v0.049 changelog

**The desktop review: tests, fairer bots, a clearer surrender and a sturdier desktop app.**

1. Automated tests ship with the desktop package: `npm test` checks every script, the content (16 heroes, real items in every build, all 59 Steam achievements), replays in all five modes (same final state and same event sequence), and simulation invariants (health, gold, cooldowns, respawn limits, match endings)
2. A seeded simulation batch runner and summary (`npm run sim`, `tests/summarize.js`) report match length percentiles, first blood and first tower, side balance, income by lane, bot play styles and hero win rates with 95% intervals. REVIEW.md now holds current results instead of v0.041/v0.042 data
3. Bots respect pagoda range when chasing: allies and easier enemies no longer follow a fleeing hero under an untanked tower, and diving needs a nearly dead target, spare health and a teammate close by. Celestial and Mythic enemies still take calculated risks
4. Laners hold their lane through the early game instead of drifting to distant bosses (objective-minded bots still go)
5. Lane economy: soldiers pay 2 more gold, the laner assigned to a lane collects the shared gold from further away, a soldier lost to a pagoda pays nearby heroes a bigger share, and jungle camps pay less and respawn after 125 s instead of 100 s
6. Surrender is now a visible vote: allies answer one by one, you can cancel it, it needs 4 of 5, and a failed or cancelled vote locks voting for a while. The first click's confirmation expires after 4 seconds. Surrenders are recorded, so replays end the same way
7. The death recap always names the final blow, even when no damage was recorded
8. Routine banners no longer stack up: a repeat within 25 seconds, or a small banner queued behind two others, goes to the kill feed
9. While dead on desktop, arrow keys and edge panning look around and the Center key follows your ally again; on phones a Follow button appears in the death bar
10. Desktop display modes: Fullscreen, Borderless and Windowed. The window remembers its size, position and monitor
11. Menus and the HUD scale up on 1440p, 4K and ultrawide screens; the main menu and shop fit a 1024×640 window
12. Settings are checked on load: a damaged settings file is set aside and defaults are used, wrong types and out-of-range values are repaired, and keys reserved for items, the scoreboard, fullscreen and the controls card can no longer be bound
13. Achievements unlocked during a match now reach Steam (they only did at the end before)
14. Steam readiness: `npm run release-check` lists what still blocks a public release (the test App ID 480, achievement icons, versions, Electron security), and 118 achievement icons (locked and unlocked, 256×256) are in build/achievements
15. The post-match coach has a button to practise its suggestion: the last-hit drill, the training dummy, replays, Bridge Battle or Celestial Rush
16. Mode cards show match lengths measured from simulated matches (Three Realms 12–21 min, Celestial Rush 8–15, Bridge 6–15, King of the River 5–8, Duel 5–12)
17. Windows builds can be made as a portable zip from any computer (`npm run dist:zip`), with the game's icon and version on the .exe
18. Replays from earlier versions are flagged as incompatible (simulation version 44)
