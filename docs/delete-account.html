Delete your account

Heavenly Mandate (天命) · by Xianwuli

English简体中文Bahasa Indonesia

You can delete your Heavenly Mandate online account and its data at any time, in either of two ways.

In the game (instant)

  1. Open Settings › Online.
  2. Tap Delete my online account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted at once.

By email (no game needed)

Send an email to heavenlymandatesupport@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account and your player name (shown in the game under Online, for example Name#1234). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your online account, player name, ratings and ranks, match history and friends list. Your name is also removed from saved match records.

What is kept: nothing from your account. Match records that included you stay for the other players, without your name.

On your device: offline progress stays on your phone until you uninstall the game.

See the full privacy policy.

删除账号

你可以随时通过以下任一方式删除《天命》的在线账号及其数据。

在游戏中（立即生效）

  1. 打开 设置 › 联机对战。
  2. 点击 删除我的在线账号 并确认。

通过邮件（无需打开游戏）

发送邮件至 heavenlymandatesupport@gmail.com，主题写 Delete my account，并附上你的玩家名称（例如 名字#1234）。我们会在 30 天内删除并通过邮件确认。

删除内容：在线账号、玩家名称、评分和段位、对局记录及好友列表；保存的对局记录中的名字也会被移除。设备上的离线进度会保留，直到你卸载游戏。

Hapus akunmu

Kamu bisa menghapus akun online Heavenly Mandate dan datanya kapan saja, dengan salah satu cara berikut.

Di dalam game (langsung)

  1. Buka Setelan › Online.
  2. Ketuk Hapus akun Online-ku, lalu konfirmasi.

Lewat email (tanpa membuka game)

Kirim email ke heavenlymandatesupport@gmail.com dengan subjek Delete my account dan nama pemainmu (misalnya Nama#1234). Kami menghapusnya dalam 30 hari dan mengonfirmasi lewat email.

Yang dihapus: akun online, nama pemain, rating dan rank, riwayat pertandingan, dan daftar teman; namamu juga dihapus dari catatan pertandingan yang tersimpan. Progres offline tetap di ponselmu sampai game dicopot.
