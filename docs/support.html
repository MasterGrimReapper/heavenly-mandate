Support

We read every message. English, 中文 and Bahasa Indonesia are all welcome.

Email: heavenlymandatesupport@gmail.com

Discord: join our community server to ask questions, report a bug or open a private support ticket.

Please include your player name (shown in the game under Online, for example Name#1234), your phone model and what happened. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.

Common questions

The game says it needs an update. Update from Google Play, or, if you installed the APK from this site, open the game and tap Update when it asks.

Online play says the server is being updated. Your game is newer than the server for a few minutes after a release. Offline play works meanwhile, and online opens on its own.

The game runs slowly on my phone. Settings › Graphics › choose a lower preset, or turn on "Stay cool automatically".

I want to delete my account. See Delete your account.

Privacy. See the privacy policy.
