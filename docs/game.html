The Game

Two teams of five immortals, three lanes, a jungle full of monsters, and a Heart of Heaven to break. Here is how Heavenly Mandate plays.

战

How a match is won

Push down a lane, destroy its three pagoda towers and its Seal, then the two guardian pagodas, then the enemy Heart of Heaven.

  • Soldiers march down three lanes. Between them lies a jungle of monsters, river bosses and the great Kunpeng.
  • Gold from soldiers, monsters and takedowns buys items at the Celestial Market. Levels unlock your ultimate and make your troop stronger.
  • Shrines, watch towers, peach groves and cloud gates give a team the edge, and every battlefield has an objective of its own.
  • Out of combat for five seconds, immortals rest and slowly regain health and Qi.
兵

Every immortal brings troops

Besides four abilities and a passive, each immortal summons a troop: Celestial Hounds for Erlang Shen, Sun Crows for Hou Yi, the Jade Rabbit and the Moon Rabbits for Chang'e.

  • Your troop follows you and fights at your side. Press the troop button again to send it at your target.
  • Troops evolve at levels 6, 11 and 16.
  • Some items strengthen your troops, and bosses can grant troop upgrades on certain battlefields.
网

Online: against real players

Matchmaking by skill on our own server. Every phone runs the same battle in lockstep, and the server checks every result. If a player leaves, a bot takes over, and they can reconnect.

地

Seven battlefields

The same war on seven landscapes, each with its own objective and living weather.

翠谷

Jade Valley

Spring in the river valley: pine and cherry blossom, bamboo groves and a clear jade river.

龙Dragon Gate. From 8:00 a golden gate rises on the river at the heart of the valley. Hold it for 8 seconds: the carp leaps, and every troop of your team gains an extra soldier for the rest of the match.

Living weather: spring showers shorten vision

昆仑

Kunlun Snowfield

High on sacred Kunlun: snow-laden pines, frozen streams and ice crystals in the wind.

雪Avalanche Shrines. From 7:00 two shrines wake where the river meets the side lanes. Capture one to send an avalanche down that lane: it buries enemy soldiers, stuns heroes and freezes their pagoda for 15 seconds.

Living weather: deep snow slows you off the roads

火焰山

Flaming Mountains

The burning range from Journey to the West: red stone, ember-lit groves and a river of lava.

扇Fire-Fan Altar. From 6:00 the Banana-Leaf Fan rests on an altar at the river's end. Claim it: your team's attacks burn for 75 seconds and the enemy's outer pagodas are scorched, taking 40% more damage for a minute.

Living weather: the lava river burns

枫谷

Maple Gorge

Autumn in the gorge: blazing maples, golden ginkgo and drifting leaves.

鹿Spirit Deer. From 7:30 a golden spirit deer roams the jungle, and it runs when hurt. Bring it down for the Antler Blessing: your troops gain +30% health and damage and come back twice as fast for two minutes.

Living weather: autumn showers shorten vision

灯夜

Lantern Night

The Lantern Festival: a moonlit valley, every lantern lit and sky lanterns rising.

灯Lantern Capture. From 4:00 lanterns drift down across the valley. Stand on one to claim it; every fifth lantern lights the sky, revealing every enemy for 20 seconds and paying each hero 150 gold.

Living weather: the night shortens vision

敦煌

Dunhuang Sands

An oasis on the Silk Road: golden dunes, poplar groves, cave shrines and a turquoise river.

驼Sandstorm Caravan. Every five minutes from 6:00 a camel caravan crosses the sands along the river. Raid it for 200 gold each; its sandstorm then blinds the other team (shorter vision, no minimap) for 45 seconds.

Living weather: sandstorms hide the minimap

天宫

Heavenly Palace

The Jade Emperor's court above the clouds: white jade terraces, golden roofs and a river of cloud. The Southern Gate stands at its heart.

门Southern Gate. From 7:00 the Southern Gate stands open at the river's heart. Hold it for 8 seconds and Heaven's soldiers march for you: a Heavenly General joins each lane for three waves and your heroes strike structures 15% harder.

Living weather: drifting clouds shorten vision

友

Friends, parties and chat

  • Add a friend by their name, like Name#1234. See who is online, in a party or in a match. Up to 100 friends.
  • Invite friends into a party of up to five. The leader picks a queue and a format, and the party searches together, always on the same team.
  • Team, all and party chat, with four quick phrases. Messages are filtered for bad words, limited to one a second and never saved. Hold a name to mute that player.
  • Friends and your party live on the main menu, so you can invite and press Play together without opening Online.
师

The first lesson

An old master teaches the basics in a short, playable lesson, one action at a time.

  • The screen dims except the button to press, a drawn finger shows the tap or the drag, and an arrow points to every target.
  • Each step waits until you have done it, and you cannot die during the lesson.
  • Then a six-stop tour of the menu and five beginner quests with rewards. Already know MOBAs? Skip it, and replay it any time from Settings.
  • After a match, the coach suggests a short lesson in the Practice Grounds when it spots something to work on.
言

Three languages

The whole game in English, Simplified Chinese and Indonesian. On first start it picks your phone’s language; change it any time in Settings › Gameplay › Language. Immortals speak in English or Mandarin.
